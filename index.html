what we do

The Orizon Software Foundation is a small non-profit that maintains infrastructure libraries, developer tooling, and reference implementations for open protocols. We believe software should be free, open source, and reliable. No user should have to worry about spyware or malware with software that is free to use, free to modify, free to inspect.

"Free software is a matter of liberty, not price. To understand the concept, you should think of 'free' as in 'free speech,' not as in 'free beer.'"
- Richard Stallman

We don't take venture funding. We don't have a blockchain strategy. We write C, Assembly, Rust, and Python, and we ship code that runs on hardware you probably own.

bring your project under Orizon

A lot of people want to make free software, not join an organization. We get that.

Independent FOSS projects can put themselves under the Orizon umbrella while staying fully independent. You keep your own governance, license, repositories, and direction. We simply give you a roof and some useful infrastructure for three years until your project can mature.

what you get for three years

  • A free email address under the orizonsoftwarefoundation.org domain
  • Free hosting: up to 120 GB storage, 24 GB RAM, 8 vCores, and 500 Mbps – 1 Gbps networking
  • Optional listing on this page if you want visibility
  • Access to our Matrix room and the small community around the foundation

We ensure your project stays yours. Once you're under our umbrella, your project stays fully independent. You can choose to opt out at any time if your experience and/or our services do not meet your standards.

If this sounds useful, write to contact@orizonsoftwarefoundation.org
and tell us about your project.
